Instructions

In this task, you will face a series of 16 coin-toss trials.

For each trial, imagine a coin is tossed:

  • If the coin lands Heads, you win a certain amount of money.
  • If the coin lands Tails, you lose a certain amount of money.

For each trial, you must decide whether to Accept (play the gamble) or Reject (skip it).

There are no right or wrong answers. please respond as quickly and honestly as you can.

The task takes about 2–3 minutes.
